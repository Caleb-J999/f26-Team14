## Description
> Studio Share is a platform designed for small groups that need short-term access to specialized creative spaces.
> The app makes renting creative spaces more practical by allowing users to find multiple available spaces in one place instead of searching across different websites or locations.
> The goal of Studio Share is to make the right creative spaces quicker and easier to find while also helping space owners make their available spaces easier to discover and book.

## App Functions

1. Customer/Renter:
   1. Create/modify customer profile - Users can create an account and manage their personal information.
   2. View available spaces - Users can search and browse available creative spaces.
   3. Book available time slots - Users can check availability and reserve an open time slot for a space.
   4. View/manage bookings - Users can view and manage their current and upcoming reservations.

2. Provider/Space Owner:
   1. Create/modify/remove provider profile - Space owners can create and manage their provider account.
   2. Create and edit listings - Providers can create listings for their available creative spaces and update listing information.
   3. Manage availability - Providers can specify when their spaces are available for booking.
   4. View and manage bookings - Providers can view and manage reservations made for their spaces.

3. SysAdmin:
   1. Manage user access - Administrators can manage customer and provider accounts.
   2. Moderate space listings - Administrators can review and remove inappropriate or misleading listings.
   3. Monitor platform activity - Administrators can monitor overall activity on the platform.
   4. Remove inappropriate content - Administrators can remove content that violates platform rules.
